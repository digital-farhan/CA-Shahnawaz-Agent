## Priority-service coverage gaps observed (factual note, not a scored finding)

Per website-crawl.md's instruction that "a missing page for a priority service is itself worth noting," the following CLAUDE.md priority services do not appear to have a dedicated URL in the current sitemap/navigation (based on what was discoverable this session — not confirmed absent via admin access):

- **Company Registration (private limited specifically)** — only a Public Limited Company page was found; no dedicated Private Limited Company registration page.
- **LLP Registration** — only "LLP Annual Filing" (a compliance page) was found; no dedicated LLP formation/registration page.
- **Partnership Registration** — only a Tax Guides blog article ("Partnership Firm Registration in Maharashtra") exists; no dedicated service page.
- **Proprietorship Registration** — no dedicated page found.
- **Tax Consultancy / Income Tax Compliance / GST Consultancy / GST Compliance / Internal Audit** — these are named as distinct priority services in CLAUDE.md but no separately dedicated pages were found (Income Tax Return Filing and Audit Services pages may cover some of this ground implicitly).
- **Company Compliance / Annual Compliance (Private Limited / OPC)** — the contact form's "Select Service" dropdown lists "Private Limited Company Annual Compliances," "Annual Compliance for OPC," and "Section-8 Annual Compliance" as selectable options (so the firm captures leads for these), but no dedicated landing page for any of them was found in the sitemap or navigation.

This is recorded as a discovery-stage observation only, per website-crawl.md's scope; severity/priority scoring of these gaps belongs to content-audit.md / technical-seo.md in a later phase.
