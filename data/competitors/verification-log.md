## Competitive pattern routed into the audit

1. Make the practising CA and firm credentials explicit on the homepage, About page, and money pages.
2. Use a clear process, deliverables, timeline and fee/quote rationale on each commercial page.
3. Build owned pages for company registration, LLP registration, ROC/company compliance, GST compliance/consultancy and internal audit instead of leaving these intents absent or redirecting externally.
4. Keep query-specific local pages useful and differentiated; do not copy mass location-page patterns without genuine local relevance.
